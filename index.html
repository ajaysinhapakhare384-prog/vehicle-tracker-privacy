Vehicle Maintenance Tracker

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Vehicle Maintenance Tracker ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") helps users manage their vehicles, maintenance records, fuel logs, expenses, and reminders.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or sign in to an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Name and profile information, if provided by your sign-in provider
  • Firebase Authentication user ID
  • Authentication-related information required to manage your account

The App supports Google Sign-In and Email/Password authentication through Firebase Authentication.

Vehicle and Maintenance Information

When you use the App, you may provide information such as:

  • Vehicle name, make, model, and registration details
  • Vehicle maintenance and service records
  • Fuel records and fuel expenses
  • Other vehicle-related expenses
  • Reminder information and maintenance schedules
  • Additional information you choose to enter

This information is associated with your account and stored using Firebase services to support the App's features.

Account Deletion Requests

If you submit an account deletion request, we may collect:

  • Your Firebase user ID
  • Email address
  • Display name, if available
  • Additional details you provide
  • Request status and submission timestamp

These requests are stored in a Firestore collection and reviewed by the app team. Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account or associated data.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide vehicle management features
  • Save and display vehicle, maintenance, fuel, and expense records
  • Manage reminders and schedules
  • Respond to account deletion requests
  • Maintain and improve the App
  • Protect the App and its users

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

The App uses services provided by Google Firebase, including:

  • Firebase Authentication: For account registration and sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore: For storing account-related information, vehicle records, and account deletion requests.

Information stored through Firebase is subject to Google's applicable security and privacy practices.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers, including Firebase, as necessary to operate the App and provide its features.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect the security and rights of users and the App.

5. Data Security

We use Firebase security features and access controls to help protect user information.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, maintain user accounts, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Account deletion requests may remain stored while they are being reviewed and processed.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can submit an account deletion request through the App's Privacy section.

The app team reviews these requests and may contact the user if additional information is required.

Once a request is verified and processed, the user's account and associated personal data will be deleted, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Submitting a request does not immediately delete the account or data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
